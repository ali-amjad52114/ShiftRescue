# Close the Loop — A Voice AI Hackathon

Build a voice agent that gets a real task done — and prove it.

12 hours · Teams of 1–3 · AI-assisted coding fully allowed · Powered by a1mobile

---

## The One-Line Version

Anyone can wire up speech-to-text → LLM → text-to-speech in an hour. That's not the game. The game is the action layer: a voice agent that operates against systems and people it doesn't control, completes a real task, and stays honest when reality pushes back.

The loop only closes when we can independently see the result — the SMS that actually landed, the booking that actually hit the system. Not what the agent says happened. What did.

---

## What You'll Build

A voice agent that completes a real-world task involving a party it doesn't control — a human, a phone tree, or a live system — and produces a verifiable side effect we can confirm on our end.

Inbound (your agent answers) or outbound (your agent calls out) — your choice.

Pick a task, or pitch your own:
- **📅 Book / reserve / schedule** — a table, an appointment, a service slot — and confirm it
- **🔁 Change / cancel / dispute** — modify an order, cancel a subscription, reschedule
- **🎯 Get-it-done errand** — an underspecified goal across 2–3 tools ("get 4 people a dinner reservation downtown around 7 and text the group the details")
- **💬 Extract / negotiate** — call to get a quote, hours, or availability, and report it back accurately

### The Twist: Planted Friction
Every agent faces the same planted friction during judging:
- The first option is unavailable.
- A payment fails.
- The request is ambiguous.
- The person on the other end interrupts and mumbles.

Your agent has to adapt, offer alternatives, and stay honest — a confident lie loses to an honest "I couldn't do that."

## How You're Scored

Judged on a live call we run against your agent — a fresh scenario with the same planted friction for everyone. We score the side effect we can confirm, not the agent's claims.

> ⚠️ **One hard rule**: a fabricated success is an automatic critical flag. If your agent says "all booked!" and nothing happened on our end, it can't place — no matter how good it sounded. Honesty about failure beats a confident lie, every time.

## Rules That Keep It Fair & Legal

1. **AI tooling is unlimited** — copilots, agents, any model, any framework. Assumed, not cheating.
2. **Controlled endpoints only**. Every counterparty is provided by us — a number we answer or a sandbox we run. No cold-calling real businesses or people. No real financial transactions. This keeps it legal, ethical, and fair — everyone faces the identical planted friction.
3. **The side effect must be real.** "Pretend it sent" doesn't count.
4. **No pre-recorded or Wizard-of-Oz demos.** Judging is live, against a scenario you haven't seen.
5. **Original work during the event.** Starter kits and open-source frameworks are fine; pre-built task-specific agents are not.
